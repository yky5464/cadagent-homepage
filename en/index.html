Edit CAD drawings by asking

CADAgent is a Windows program that reads DWG and DXF drawings. Ask in plain language, for example "change line 013-PP-4001 to 65A", and its generative AI finds the objects and edits them. It shows what it will change first, and saves only after you confirm. AI can make mistakes, so check the result before you use it.

DWG · DXF

Read and edit drawings

Finds and reads text, dimensions and the contents of blocks, and edits them. Hand it repetitive work such as checking line numbers, cleaning up duplicate objects or counting sizes.

Multiple sheets

Many sheets at once

Follows a line across the sheets of a set, such as a P&ID, and edits them together. It shows the plan first and asks you to confirm each sheet before saving.

Pro · AutoCAD

In the drawing open in AutoCAD

Connects to AutoCAD on the same PC and works in the drawing you have open, so each change appears in AutoCAD as it is made. If you edit the drawing yourself between requests, CADAgent sees your changes before it continues.

Pro · Standards and public data

Check against standards

Looks up the Korean construction standards (KDS · KCS), laws, the building register and public procurement prices, and compares them with what the drawing says.
